Adaptations (scripts/prepare_topbrain_subject.py, scripts/convert_subject.py, scripts/splat_context.py):
- case.glb + case.json: each label value became one closed surface mesh on the native 0.51 x 0.51 x 0.625 mm voxel grid, without registration, resampling or smoothing; voxel -> NIfTI affine -> RAS mm -> (x, z, -y) / 1000 glTF metres, Y-up. Display colours, opacities and the anterior / posterior circulation groupings are this project's, not part of the release. Per-structure label values, voxel counts and checksums are in case.json.
- case.context.ply + case.context.json: the CTA intensities as one Gaussian per selected voxel, initialised directly from the volume (no fitting), in the same glTF frame. Colour and opacity are a transfer function of Hounsfield units: bone >= 300 HU, contrast 150-300 HU, soft tissue -200-150 HU. To keep the download near 33 MB the layer was built with a 650,000-splat budget: bone keeps every 2nd voxel per axis, contrast every 2nd, soft tissue every 4th (624,246 splats). It is a reduced rendering of the scan, not the scan itself and not a photograph.
- Nothing was mirrored, sculpted or inferred; an absent structure means it lies outside the scan field of view or was not annotated, not that the anatomy is absent.

This is one subject of a research dataset shown for anatomy education. It is not a diagnostic device and not evidence about any patient.
